Field report

The integration product behind 150+ enterprise rollouts

The client sold expense and invoice automation into enterprises whose finance data lived in SAP. Every deal turned on whether the integration would hold, and no two landscapes were alike: ECC 6.0 in one account, S/4HANA in the next, private cloud, public cloud, and somebody else’s middleware in between.

I architected and delivered the full integration product suite. One multi-tenant product rather than a connector per customer, spanning ECC 6.0 and S/4HANA across private and public cloud, through CPI, PI/PO and third-party middleware, posting into FI-CO and MM and reading from HR and HCM. The reliability rebuild did three things: posting became idempotent and queued, master data was validated before a document ever left, and failures went from terminal to classified and reprocessable.

Organizationally it meant running two SAP-side teams of functional and development consultants, split by solution path because public cloud and ABAP are different disciplines, coordinating throughout with the internal integrations team.
